How auto-calibration works

  1. Projector turns black and OpenCV detects the physical Go board.
  2. The projector flashes known dots one at a time.
  3. OpenCV finds each dot in camera coordinates using frame differencing.
  4. RANSAC solves the camera ↔ projector homography.
  5. The physical board corners are transformed into projector coordinates.
  6. A 19×19 test grid is projected exactly over the board.

The sequential-dot approach is intentionally tolerant of keystone, projector angle, different board sizes, and points falling outside the webcam view.
