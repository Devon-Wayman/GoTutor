Startup Workflow

  1. The external projector opens with a gray/white illumination field.
  2. Start the camera and position the complete Go board in view.
  3. OpenCV detects the physical board.
  4. Once board confidence is sufficient, Begin Calibration is enabled.
  5. Calibration switches the projector to black and performs coarse/dense projected-point scans.
  6. The solved 19×19 grid appears briefly for visual verification.
  7. The projector automatically returns to black for gameplay/stone tracking.

If the board is difficult to detect, increase or decrease the illumination slider until its outer boundary and grid are clearly visible in the camera.
